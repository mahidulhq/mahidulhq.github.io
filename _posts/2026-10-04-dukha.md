---
title: "Dhukha"
date: 2026-10-04 01:00:00 +0600
categories: [diary]
---

Dil dukha hai aaj hamaara, kya baat likhi hai,  
Aapne yeh kaisi dard-e-shaam likhi hai.  
Aasmaan jo gira hai mere pairon mein aaj,  
Iski tabaahi ki tarkeeb maine khud hi ki hai.  
Bakwaas nahi yeh lafz, yeh mohabbat fana nahi,  
Khuda kal bhi tha, Khuda aaj bhi hai.  
Bas ek hum aur aap hain, jo is shayari ke peeche guzar jaayenge.
